Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Oh Crap! Run! ("the Game") is developed and published by HelioShred Games ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the Game uses, who receives it, and how you can control it.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. The Game has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no ads. We do not run servers that receive information about you or your games.

The Game stores a few things locally on your device so it works the way you left it:

  • Your progress: the scenes you have reached, your best distances and medals
  • Your achievements and play counts (such as runs played and total distance)
  • Your settings (narrator, music, sound effects, vibration, camera)
  • Whether you have already learned to jump and slide
  • Any scenes or items you have purchased

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS), the Game reports your achievements and submits your furthest run to the Game's leaderboard. Your distance appears on the leaderboard next to your Game Center nickname, where other players can see it. Game Center is run by Apple under its own privacy policy. You can play without signing in; your progress is then kept on your device only.

3. Replay Clips

After a run, the Game can record a short replay clip of your last moments. The clip is made and kept on your device. It is only shared if you tap Share and choose an app in your device's share sheet. We never receive your clips or anything you share.

4. In-App Purchases

The Game may offer optional one-time purchases, such as unlocking scenes. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store payment information. Purchases can be restored at no cost from the Game's Settings on any device signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

5. Fonts

The Game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your Choices

  • Game Center: sign out of Game Center to stop reporting achievements and distances.
  • Local data: uninstalling the Game deletes everything it stored on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child's data has been collected through the Game, please contact us and we will help get it removed.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add ads to a future version. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
