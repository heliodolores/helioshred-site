Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Your dung ball is rolling after you! Drag your finger left and right to steer the beetle, tap or swipe up to jump and swipe down to dive. Grab dung pellets: every one you miss makes the ball bigger. Collect 35 to charge the fart dash, then tap 💨 to blast ahead and smash through everything. Open Settings → How to play any time for a quick guide.

How do I unlock new worlds?

Run far enough to reach a world and its challenge opens: play it from Challenges and win its bronze, silver and gold medals. You can also try any world for free; after that, watch a short ad to try it again, or unlock it in the Shop.

How do I turn off the narrator, the music or the vibration?

Open Settings on the main menu: the narrator, music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch.

How do I restore my purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy them, then tap Restore purchases in Settings or in the Shop.

My achievements or distance aren't in Game Center.

They are reported when you're signed in to Game Center. Anything you earned while signed out is sent automatically the next time you open the Game signed in. Only your furthest run appears on the leaderboard.

How do I remove the ads?

The Remove Ads purchase in the Shop removes every ad: the second chance after a fall and extra tries of locked worlds become free, no video needed. Without it, ads only play when you choose to watch one.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). See our Privacy Policy, section 6, for tracking choices.

The sound stopped after I locked my phone.

Tap the screen once: the Game restarts its sound. If it stays silent, check your device's volume and the silent switch.

How do I share a replay?

After a run, tap Replay & share, pick a camera angle and a length, and tap Share when the clip is ready.
