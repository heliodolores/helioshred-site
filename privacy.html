Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

HelioShred Games ("we", "us", "our") makes small mobile games for iOS and Android. This policy covers our website, helioshred.com, and how we handle email you send us. Each of our games also has its own privacy policy, listed below, which describes exactly what that game uses.

1. Our Games

Please read the policy for the game you play:

  • FlipP4THflipp4th.app/privacy
  • Cool Divecooldive.app/privacy
  • Oh Crap! Run!helioshred.com/ohcrap/privacy

In short, our games have no accounts or sign-up. Progress and settings are stored on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob and its partners, which may collect device and ad-interaction data under their own policies, with consent requested where the law requires it. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we never see payment details. Where a game uses anonymous analytics, its policy says so and explains how to turn them off.

2. This Website

helioshred.com is a static website. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising, and we do not track you across pages or other sites.

Two third-party services are involved when you load a page:

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

We do not receive or have access to this information.

Links to app stores, our game websites and other services take you to sites with their own privacy practices.

3. Emailing Us

If you email support@helioshred.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We do not add you to mailing lists, sell your information or share it with anyone, except where required by law.

We keep support emails only as long as they are useful for helping you, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

4. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we hold very little, this usually means support emails. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

For data collected by ad networks, Apple or Google, use the choices described in the relevant game's policy or contact those companies directly.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games and website are not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
